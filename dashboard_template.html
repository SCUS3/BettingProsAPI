Gap: projection minus line, as a % of the line. Score: 40% edge (gap in standard deviations) + 35% EV at FanDuel's price + 25% matchup. Balanced: 40% score + 25% recent form + 20% market value + 15% BettingPros rating. Both scores are cut for DTD (×0.85). Learned: the model's own score from graded results, blended with balanced until it has enough history.
Proj*: placeholder projection (a whole number exactly 0.5 off the line). It claims no edge, its EV uses FanDuel's no-vig price, and it ranks below every real projection unless the price alone is +EV. DvP: opponent defense vs. position, 1 = softest. Form: how often the recommended side hit this line recently. Kelly: quarter-Kelly stake, 1u = 1% of bankroll; 0u means no edge at this price.
